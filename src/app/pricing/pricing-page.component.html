Pricing

What it costs

Joining is free. You pay the trainer or center for sessions, and Berliz plans unlock more. Every price is in Canadian dollars (CAD).

Sessions

Set by each trainer and center

Berliz does not mark up sessions. Each trainer and center sets their own rate and shows it on their profile.

Single session

An hourly rate, prorated by session length. You are charged only after the provider confirms, with 24 hours to pay.

Packs and monthly plans

Buy several sessions at once or subscribe monthly with a trainer or center. Prices are on the Level up page of their profile.

Cancelling

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before a paid session; inside that window 50% is refunded. A provider may set their own terms, shown on their profile.
